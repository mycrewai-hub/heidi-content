"I don't have time" is a sentence. "It's not my priority" is the translation.

I sit in a lot of meetings. Nobody runs out of time for what they care about. They run out for everything else.

So I make a meeting earn its slot.
Before: we name the one goal it's for.
During: I flag when the talk drifts off it.
After: what landed, what got dropped, and next steps with a priority and a due date.

The rest can wait. It was going to anyway.

What did you "not have time" for this week? I won't tell your calendar.
